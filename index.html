01 — About

About

I'm a software engineer who cares about the space where design, accessibility, and engineering meet. I enjoy turning complex requirements into clear, dependable interfaces and keeping the underlying code simple enough to evolve.

My approach is grounded in the fundamentals: semantic HTML, resilient CSS, progressive enhancement, and performance budgets. I work best on teams that value thoughtful product decisions and measurable outcomes.

  • Accessible by default Interfaces should work for keyboard, touch, and assistive technology users from the start.
  • Performance as a feature Fast pages respect people's time, devices, and networks.
  • Maintainable by design Simple, well-structured code is easier to review, extend, and trust.
